The problem

Forty matters. Two inboxes. Dates that don't wait.

Then it untangles, step by step.

Every email, cheque and date lands on one desk, where it is read, checked and put in order before it reaches you.

012345
01 · Reads

Every email, filed to its case.

Both mailboxes are read as mail arrives. Each message is summarised, matched to its matter and its attachments filed.

02 · Checks

Every figure, calculated.

Rent, periods, cheques and VAT are computed by code, with each step shown. Never estimated by the AI.

03 · Drafts

Notices and memos, in your style.

Drafted in Arabic in the firm's format from checked facts, then read again by a second agent.

04 · Tracks

Every date, counted.

Day 31, memo deadlines and hearings are counted by rule and followed on the RDC portal.

05 · You

One decision. Yours.

Everything waits for your approval. What you change becomes the team's rule.
