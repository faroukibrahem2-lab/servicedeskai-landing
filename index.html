Our first desk

Rental Desk AI

A working platform for rental disputes at the Rental Disputes Centre. A team of specialised agents follows each case from the first email to the update for the client or owner, in Arabic and English, while you stay in charge.

Now in a private pilot at a Dubai law firm. Every figure is calculated by code, every fact points to its source, and every action is recorded with who approved it.

  1. An email arrivesThe Mail Desk reads it and matches it to the right case.
  2. Facts and figures are checkedLease, Ejari and cheques are read; every amount and period is re-calculated.
  3. The document is draftedLegal notices and memos in the firm's own Arabic or English style.
  4. You approveNothing is served, filed or sent without your decision.
  5. The case is trackedRDC updates, hearings and deadlines, followed automatically.
  6. The client or owner hears backA clear update goes out once you agree.
